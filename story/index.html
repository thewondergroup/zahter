01Hikâyemiz/Our Story

A family story, cooked over fire

Zahter began with one chef's love for the food of Istanbul. Today her family carries it on — just off Carnaby Street.

Esra Muslu, founding chef

Where it began

Esra's Istanbul, in London

Zahter is the vision of chef Esra Muslu. Born and raised on the food of Istanbul, Esra spent her career cooking across Istanbul and London before opening Zahter on Foubert's Place — her first solo restaurant in London.

She wanted a place that felt like a Turkish home: generous plates for the middle of the table, bread from the wood oven, and cooking that respects the ingredient. Contemporary, but rooted in Istanbul's heritage.

In loving memory

Esra Muslu

Esra built Zahter to share the food of the city she grew up in. She is deeply missed, and her recipes, her standards and her spirit still guide everything we cook and every guest we welcome.

Founding chef / her legacy continues

The Zahter kitchen

Zahter today

Carried on by family

Today Zahter is run by Esra's sister, Yasemin Efe, and their family, alongside partner Simay Kamer of QFood.

The kitchen still cooks over wood and charcoal, the menu still follows the seasons, and the welcome is still pure Turkish hospitality — warm, attentive and generous. Breakfast, lunch and dinner, every day.
